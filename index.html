Privacy Policy — Spirit Wanderers: Endless Trail

Last updated: 13 November 2026

"Spirit Wanderers: Endless Trail" (the "Game") is published by Pr Pro Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

1. Summary

We do not sell or share your information, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or advertising ID. The Game has no account system. To keep the Game fair, it sends gameplay records and a random identifier to our own server, as described in section 3.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, language choice, purchases/rewards state and similar game data are saved in the Game's private storage on your device. This saved data is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. A copy of the game progress is included in the gameplay records described in section 3.

3. Fair-play checks and gameplay data

After each finished battle the Game sends a record of that battle to our server: the moves that were made, the game progress at that moment (for example level, gold and items), the Game version, and a random identifier created by the Game on your device. The identifier is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID or location. When it starts, the Game also asks our server which version is the oldest one still supported; this request contains no information about you.

We use this data only to detect cheating (fraud prevention) and to analyse and balance the Game. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), stored on our server hosted by Fly.io, and is not sold or shared with third parties. To ask for the data linked to your device to be deleted, write to the address in section 9.

4. Local multiplayer ("Play with friends")

This mode connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly to each other. The Game sends small network broadcast messages to find nearby rooms and exchanges gameplay data with the connected friend. This data is not sent to us or to any server on the internet. The Game may request network permissions only for this purpose.

5. Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version contains no third-party advertising and no real-money purchases. If we add them in a future update (for example Google Play Billing or Google AdMob), those services may collect device or advertising identifiers and purchase information under their own privacy policies, and we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety form before release.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

7. Permissions

The Game uses only the permissions needed to run and to use the network (for local multiplayer and for the fair-play checks in section 3). It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version and date are always shown on this page.
