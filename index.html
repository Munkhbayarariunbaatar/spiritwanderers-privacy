Privacy Policy — Spirit Wanderers: Endless Trail

Last updated: 13 November 2026

"Spirit Wanderers: Endless Trail" (the "Game") is published by Pr Pro Production ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

1. Summary

We do not sell or share your information, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or advertising ID. You never have to register or type a password: the Game creates a random game account by itself. To keep the Game fair and to keep your progress safe, it sends gameplay records, a copy of your progress and that random identifier to our own server (section 3). If you use Google Play Games, the Game can use it to find your progress again on a new phone (section 4).

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, language choice, purchases/rewards state and similar game data are saved in the Game's private storage on your device. This saved data is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. A copy of the game progress is also kept on our server, as described in section 3.

3. Fair-play checks, cloud save and gameplay data

After each finished battle the Game sends a record of that battle to our server: the moves that were made, the game progress at that moment (for example level, gold and items), the Game version, and a random identifier created by the Game on your device. The identifier is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID or location. When it starts, the Game also asks our server which version is the oldest one still supported; this request carries the same random identifier and the Game version.

Cloud save. While you play, the Game regularly sends a copy of your game progress (the same data that is saved on your device) to our server, stored under the random identifier, so that it can be restored if you reinstall the Game or change phones. The name you type in the Game is not sent to the server.

We use this data only to detect cheating (fraud prevention), to keep and restore your progress, and to analyse and balance the Game. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), stored on our server hosted by Fly.io, and is not sold or shared with third parties. To have it deleted, see section 9.

4. Google Play Games sign-in

If you have a Google Play Games profile, the Game signs in with it automatically; if you do not, the Game works the same without it. After sign-in, Google gives our server your Play Games player ID. We store only a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of that ID, linked to your random game account, and use it for one purpose: to find your account and saved progress when you install the Game again or on another phone. We never receive your Google email address, real name or contacts, we do not keep your Play Games display name or picture, and nothing about your play is posted to Google. Google Play Games is provided by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Local multiplayer ("Play with friends")

This mode connects devices on the same Wi-Fi network directly to each other. The Game sends small network broadcast messages to find nearby rooms and exchanges gameplay data with the connected friend. This data is not sent to us or to any server on the internet. The Game may request network permissions only for this purpose.

6. Advertising and in-app purchases

The current version contains no third-party advertising and no real-money purchases. If we add them in a future update (for example Google Play Billing or Google AdMob), those services may collect device or advertising identifiers and purchase information under their own privacy policies, and we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety form before release.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Permissions

The Game uses only the permissions needed to run and to use the network (for local multiplayer, Google Play Games sign-in and the server features in section 3). It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

9. Deleting your data

To have your game account and everything stored with it on our server deleted (gameplay records, saved progress and the Google Play Games link), write to munkhbayarariunbaatar@gmail.com from any address and tell us you want your Spirit Wanderers data deleted. We will reply with how to identify your game account and delete the data within 30 days. Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version and date are always shown on this page.

11. Contact

Questions: munkhbayarariunbaatar@gmail.com
